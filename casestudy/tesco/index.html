01 — The brief

“Build a solution which can eliminate the multiple charges associated with payment transactions.”

Every card payment at a till carries scheme, interchange and terminal costs. Worldpay asked: if the shopper's phone could replace the card — and the bank account could replace the card network — what would checkout at a store the size of Tesco look like? I researched where payments were heading, mapped a new journey and designed the app that makes it work.

Key insight

By 2018, China had already skipped cards for QR. The West was slower — cards were entrenched and only 27% of consumers were interested in wallets. The opening: make the phone the terminal, and the bank account the source of funds.

03 — Rethinking the journey

Keep the till. Take the card out of it.

2018 checkout

5 steps · card network
  1. 1Customer brings items to the till
  2. 2Items scanned at the POS
  3. 3POS shows the amount due
  4. 4Customer chooses a payment method
  5. 5Pays by card or cash

Proposed

bank-to-bank · no card fees
  1. 1Items scanned at the POS
  2. 2POS shows the amount due
  3. 3A QR code appears on screen
  4. 4Customer scans it in the app
  5. 5Authenticates with biometrics or a short code
  6. 6Instant confirmation, on phone and till

Where the money comes from

A

Wallet linked to a Tesco Bank account

Closed loop — the lowest-cost route for Tesco's own customers.

B

Any bank account tied to a registered mobile number

Opens the app beyond Tesco Bank without a card in between.

C

App-to-app redirect to the bank for authentication

Grounded in the UK Open Banking standard, launched January 2018 — redirect and decoupled authentication.

06 — Interface

Scan, confirm, done.

Sign up once and link a bank account. At the till: scan the QR, check the amount and merchant, confirm with Touch ID. Manual code entry covers screens that won't scan.

07 — Beyond checkout

One app for in-store, online and person-to-person.

Add-on features

  • Loyalty points and rewards, applied at payment
  • In-store and online ordering from the same app
  • Person-to-person transfers
  • Overseas and cross-border payments

Further out

  • Open Banking: debit the shopper's account and credit the merchant's directly
  • Printed QR codes so small merchants can skip the POS entirely
  • AR/VR store experiences — scan products on the go

08 — Questions I left the team with
