Branch onboarding and service, in one ten-minute conversation on iPad

A proof-of-concept iPad app for Bank of the West branches. Customers open accounts and get service in one guided session, with ID scanning, personalized recommendations and a banker close by.

01 — OVERVIEW

The concept

Customers expect to open an account in a branch as easily as they do in their phone’s banking app. This concept is a shared iPad a customer uses on their own, or side by side with a banker, to get from walking in to holding an active card.

The flow has four stages: Identity, Your needs, Review, Done. It uses the iPad’s camera, Face ID and Apple Pencil, and an assistant that answers questions along the way. It doesn’t stop at submit: the same session carries on into service.
